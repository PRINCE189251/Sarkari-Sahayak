Employment Linked Incentive (ELI) Scheme

Boost Employment with Financial Incentives

+

Provides financial incentives to companies for generating new employment opportunities

Key Benefits

  • ₹5,000 per employee per month for 2 years
  • Direct subsidy to employers' bank accounts
  • For new job creation in manufacturing sector
  • MSME sector focus with additional benefits
  • Skill development training support

Eligibility Criteria

  • Registered companies in India with GST
  • Minimum 10 new employees required
  • MSME and manufacturing units priority
  • Minimum 1 year employment contract
  • Government employees not eligible

Required Documents

  • Company registration certificate
  • Employee appointment letters and contracts
  • EPFO registration and contribution proof
  • Employee Aadhaar and PAN cards
  • GST registration certificate

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana

Health Insurance for Poor Families

+

Provides health insurance coverage of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization

Key Benefits

  • ₹5 lakh health coverage per family per year
  • Cashless treatment in 25,000+ empaneled hospitals
  • Pre-existing diseases covered from day 1
  • All medical expenses including surgery, medicines
  • Transportation costs for emergency covered

Eligibility Criteria

  • Families listed in SECC 2011 database
  • Rural and urban poor families identified
  • No restrictions on family size, age or gender
  • Automatic inclusion for specific occupational categories
  • Tax-paying families not eligible

Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card of family head (Mandatory)
  • Income certificate from Tehsildar
  • Address proof (Voter ID/Ration Card)
  • Passport size photographs of family members
  • Mobile number for OTP
Apply for Ayushman Bharat
